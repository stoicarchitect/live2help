## Email 1 - Welcome and What to Expect

**From:** the logged-in consultant (usually Ella)
**When:** the client moves to Client, or on "Send onboarding pack"

**Subject:** Welcome to Live 2 Help - what happens next

Hi {{contact_first_name}},

Thank you for choosing Live 2 Help. We are really pleased to be working with {{company_name}}.

To get your account set up, you will receive two emails from our Director, Daniel Brown, over the next few minutes:

1. A secure link to review and sign our Terms of Engagement and Data Sharing Agreement online
2. Your welcome pack, pricing guide and a short site form so candidates arrive prepared

Both come from clients@live2helprecruitment.co.uk, so please look out for them. Once your agreements are signed, we can start sourcing straight away.

Speak soon,

{{signature}}
