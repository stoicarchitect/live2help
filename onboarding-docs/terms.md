## 2. The Contract

2.1 These Terms constitute the contract between the Agency and the Client. They are deemed accepted by the Client on signature, or by virtue of an Introduction to, or the Engagement of, an Applicant, or the passing of any information about an Applicant to any third party following an Introduction, whichever happens first.

2.2 These Terms contain the entire agreement between the parties and, unless otherwise agreed in writing by a director of the Agency, prevail over any other terms of business or purchase conditions put forward by the Client.

2.3 No variation to these Terms is valid unless agreed in writing between the Agency and the Client, with a copy of the varied terms provided to the Client stating the date from which they apply.

2.4 These Terms may be signed electronically. An electronic signature, together with the signing record the Agency keeps (signatory name, date, time and IP address), has the same effect as a handwritten signature. These Terms may be signed in counterparts.

## 3. Fees - Permanent Placements

3.1 The Client agrees to notify the Agency immediately of any offer of an Engagement made to the Applicant, and to confirm acceptance of that offer together with details of the agreed Remuneration.

3.2 No Fee is incurred until the Applicant commences the Engagement, at which point the Agency will raise an invoice.

3.3 The Fee for an Introduction resulting in a permanent Engagement is a percentage of the Applicant's gross annual Remuneration for the first 12 months, on the following scale:

| Annual Remuneration | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £22,000 | 12% |
| £22,001 to £30,000 | 15% |
| £30,001 to £40,000 | 20% |
| £40,001 and above | 25% |

3.4 Where Remuneration falls exactly on a band boundary (£22,000, £30,000 or £40,000), the lower rate applies.

3.5 The minimum Fee for any permanent placement is the applicable percentage in clause 3.3 applied to the full-time equivalent annual National Minimum Wage for the relevant age band at the time of Engagement.

3.6 Where the actual Remuneration is not known at the time of invoicing, the Agency will calculate the Fee on the minimum Remuneration applicable for the position, having regard to information provided by the Client and to comparable market rates.

3.7 Where the Engagement is for a fixed term of less than 12 months, the Fee is applied pro rata to the Remuneration payable for the actual period. If the Engagement is extended, or the Client re-engages the Applicant within 12 months of it ending, a further Fee is payable on the additional Remuneration.

3.8 All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be added at the prevailing rate if the Agency becomes VAT registered.

## 4. Retained Search

4.1 For senior leadership, director-level and specialist roles, the Client may instruct the Agency on a Retained Search. The total Fee is 25% of the agreed annual Remuneration, payable in two stages:

| Stage | When payable | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Commitment Fee | On instruction, before the search begins | 10% of agreed Remuneration |
| Placement Fee | On the Applicant's start date | 15% of agreed Remuneration |

4.2 The Commitment Fee is non-refundable and is credited against the total Fee on a successful hire. Where the final Remuneration differs from the figure agreed at instruction, the Placement Fee is adjusted so the total Fee equals 25% of the final Remuneration.

4.3 Each Retained Search is confirmed in a written instruction setting out the role, agreed Remuneration and search timeline.

## 5. Temporary Placements

5.1 This clause applies to Engagements of a fixed duration of up to 3 months. At instruction the Client selects Option A or Option B. The selected option is confirmed in writing and cannot be changed once the Applicant has started.

**Option A - Fixed Placement Fee**

5.2 A single fixed Fee is invoiced at the end of the Applicant's second week:

| Annual Equivalent Salary | Fixed Placement Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £25,000 | £2,500 |
| £25,001 to £35,000 | £3,500 |
| £35,001 and above | £5,000 |

5.3 If the Applicant leaves before the end of their second week, no Fee is payable and the Agency will provide a replacement candidate at no charge. No replacement obligation arises where the Applicant is dismissed for gross misconduct, or the Client ends the Engagement for reasons unrelated to the Applicant's performance.

**Option B - Monthly Fee**

5.4 A monthly Fee is invoiced at the start of each calendar month of the Engagement. No replacement is provided under Option B and monthly Fees already paid are non-refundable.

| Annual Equivalent Salary | Monthly Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £25,000 | £1,000 per month |
| £25,001 to £35,000 | £1,200 per month |
| £35,001 and above | £1,500 per month |

**Conversion to permanent employment**

5.5 If the Client converts the Applicant to permanent employment under either option, a conversion Fee is payable in addition to Fees already invoiced. Under Option B, the conversion Fee replaces any further monthly invoices.

| Annual Equivalent Salary | Conversion Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £25,000 | £1,500 |
| £25,001 to £35,000 | £2,000 |
| £35,001 and above | £3,000 |

5.6 The Client must notify the Agency in writing within 5 Business Days of any decision to convert. Monthly invoicing ceases from the date written notice is received, and the conversion invoice is raised at that point.

## 6. Payment Terms

6.1 Invoices are raised on the Applicant's confirmed start date, or on the dates set out in clauses 4 and 5. Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date.

6.2 Invoices may be paid by bank transfer to the details shown on the invoice, or online using the Pay by Bank link included with the invoice. Custom payment arrangements for retained or ongoing partnership engagements may be agreed in writing before instruction.

6.3 Where an invoice remains unpaid more than 14 days after the invoice date, a late payment charge of 1% of the original invoice value is added to the outstanding balance, and a further 1% of the original invoice value is added for each full calendar month the invoice remains unpaid after that. Each charge is a fixed administration charge calculated on the original invoice value only. It is not interest and does not compound.

6.4 Where an invoice is not paid within terms, the Agency may withdraw any negotiated fee arrangement and revert to its standard published rates for that and future placements until the account is brought up to date, and may pause active searches until the overdue balance is cleared.

## 7. Replacement Guarantee - Permanent Placements

7.1 If an Applicant placed on a permanent basis leaves, or is dismissed for reasons of performance, within 3 months of their start date, the Agency will carry out one full replacement search at no additional Fee, provided that:

- the original invoice was paid in full within the agreed payment terms;
- the Client notifies the Agency in writing within 7 days of the Applicant's last working day; and
- the departure is not attributable to a change in role, redundancy, restructure, or a material change to the agreed terms of the Engagement by the Client.

7.2 The guarantee does not apply where the Applicant is dismissed for gross misconduct, or where the Applicant leaves while subject to a performance improvement plan put in place by the Client.

7.3 The replacement search is the Agency's sole remedy under this clause. No Fee is refunded.

## 8. Cancellation Fee

8.1 If, after an offer of Engagement has been made and accepted, the Client withdraws the offer for any reason before the Applicant's start date, the Client shall pay the Agency 50% of the Fee that would have been payable under clause 3.3.

8.2 This reflects the work already carried out by the Agency and the opportunity cost to the Applicant of withdrawing from other opportunities. It is payable within 14 days of the cancellation invoice.

## 9. Introductions and Confidentiality

9.1 Introductions are confidential. If the Client passes an Applicant's details to a third party and that third party engages the Applicant within 12 months of the Introduction, the Client is liable for the Fee under clause 3.3, with no entitlement to a replacement.

9.2 A Fee under clause 3.3 is payable for any Applicant engaged by the Client, directly or indirectly, within 12 months of the Agency's Introduction, whether for the role introduced or any other role.

9.3 If the Client engages a member of the Agency's own staff introduced to the Client through their work for the Agency, a Fee of 50% of that person's first-year salary with the Client is payable. No replacement guarantee applies unless agreed in writing before the start date.

## 10. Suitability and Due Diligence

10.1 The Agency endeavours to confirm each Applicant's identity, relevant experience, training, qualifications and willingness to undertake the role. Where obtained, this is shared at Introduction or confirmed in writing within 3 Business Days.

10.2 The Client remains responsible for satisfying itself as to the suitability of the Applicant, taking up references, and complying with all legal requirements, including right to work checks, work permits, medical assessments and any regulatory requirements for the role.

10.3 The Client will provide full details of the role, including the nature and location of the work, required experience, qualifications and legal authorisations, known health and safety risks, proposed start date and duration, Remuneration and pay intervals, and the notice period.
