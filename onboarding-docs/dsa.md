## 1. Purpose

1.1 Live 2 Help provides recruitment services to the Client under the Live 2 Help Terms of Engagement. To do this, the parties share personal data about job applicants and about each other's staff. This Agreement sets out what is shared, why, and how each party protects it.

1.2 The purpose of the sharing is limited to sourcing, assessing, introducing and placing candidates with the Client, arranging interviews and offers, post-placement support, and administering the account between the parties.

## 2. Roles of the Parties

2.1 Each party is an independent controller of the personal data it holds. Live 2 Help is the controller of candidate data it collects through its own sourcing, applications, talent pool and screening. The Client becomes a separate controller of any candidate data from the point it receives it.

2.2 Where the Client asks Live 2 Help to handle personal data solely on the Client's behalf and to its instructions (for example, screening applicants the Client has sourced itself), Live 2 Help acts as the Client's processor for that work, and the processing terms in the Schedule apply.

2.3 Each party is responsible for its own compliance, including having a lawful basis for its use of the data and giving the transparency information required under Articles 13 and 14 of the UK GDPR.

## 3. Data Shared

| From Live 2 Help to the Client | From the Client to Live 2 Help |
|---|---|
| Anonymised candidate profiles, followed by identified CVs once the Client confirms interest | Names, job titles and contact details of hiring managers and authorised contacts |
| Employment history, qualifications, skills and assessment notes | Role briefs, salary information and site details |
| Interview availability, salary expectations and notice periods | Interview feedback and hiring decisions |
| Right to work confirmation and referee details for placed candidates | Offer, start date and Remuneration details needed for invoicing |

3.1 Special category data (for example health or disability information) is only shared where the candidate has asked for reasonable adjustments, and then only the minimum needed.

3.2 Live 2 Help shares anonymised profiles first by default. Identified details follow only once the Client wishes to progress a candidate.

## 4. Lawful Basis

4.1 Live 2 Help relies on legitimate interests to source and introduce candidates, and on the candidate's consent where it keeps their details in its talent pool for other roles.

4.2 The Client relies on its own lawful basis, usually legitimate interests or steps taken before entering into a contract of employment, to assess and recruit candidates.

## 5. How Data Is Protected

5.1 Each party keeps shared data secure using appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access controls, password-protected systems, two-step sign-in where available, and confidentiality obligations on staff.

5.2 The Client will only use candidate data for recruitment to the role introduced, and will not pass it to any third party except as needed to make a hiring decision.

5.3 Live 2 Help uses the following service providers to deliver its services. Each is bound by data protection terms:

| Provider | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 | Email | UK / EEA |
| Google Workspace | Document storage | UK / EEA / US |
| Render | Hosting of the Live 2 Help workspace and database | US |
| Brevo | Sending emails | EEA |
| Stripe | Invoice payments (client contact data only) | UK / EEA / US |
| Anthropic | AI-assisted drafting of candidate documents | US |
| Wispr Flow | Transcription of recorded candidate calls | US |

5.4 Live 2 Help will update this list as providers change and will tell the Client of any material change.

## 6. International Transfers

6.1 Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, Live 2 Help ensures an appropriate safeguard is in place, such as UK adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. AI-Assisted Processing and Call Recording

7.1 Live 2 Help uses AI tools to help draft candidate submissions, interview preparation and match candidates to roles. Every output is reviewed by a member of the Live 2 Help team before it is sent. No decision that has a legal or similarly significant effect on a candidate is made by automated means alone.

7.2 Candidate screening calls may be recorded and transcribed with the candidate's knowledge, to produce accurate submissions. Recordings are not shared with the Client.

## 8. Retention

8.1 Live 2 Help keeps candidate data for up to 2 years from last meaningful contact, unless the candidate renews their consent or a longer period is required by law. Placement and invoicing records are kept for 6 years for accounting purposes.

8.2 The Client keeps candidate data only for as long as needed for the recruitment decision and its own legal obligations. Unsuccessful candidates' data should be deleted within 6 months of the decision.

## 9. Individuals' Rights

9.1 Each party handles requests from individuals about the data it controls. If a party receives a request that relates to data controlled by the other, it will forward it within 3 Business Days and provide reasonable help.

## 10. Data Breaches

10.1 Each party will notify the other within 48 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting shared data, give the details reasonably needed, and cooperate on any notification to the ICO or the individuals affected.

## 11. Review and Termination

11.1 This Agreement runs for as long as the Terms of Engagement are in place and either party holds data shared under it. The parties will review it at least once a year.

11.2 On termination, each party continues to protect data it holds in line with this Agreement and its retention periods.

## 12. Liability

12.1 Each party is responsible for its own breaches of data protection law and of this Agreement, and will indemnify the other against losses arising from them, subject to the limits in the Terms of Engagement. Article 82 of the UK GDPR is unaffected.

## 13. Governing Law

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales and is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

## Schedule - Processing Terms

Applies only where Live 2 Help acts as the Client's processor under clause 2.2. Live 2 Help will:

- process the personal data only on the Client's documented instructions;
- ensure everyone processing it is bound by confidentiality;
- keep it secure in line with Article 32 of the UK GDPR;
- only use the service providers listed in clause 5.3, and give notice of any new provider so the Client can object;
- help the Client respond to individuals' rights requests and meet its obligations under Articles 32 to 36;
- delete or return the data at the end of the work, at the Client's choice, unless law requires it to be kept; and
- make available the information needed to show compliance and allow reasonable audits on notice.
