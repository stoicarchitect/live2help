Welcome to Live 2 Help Recruitment. We are delighted to be working with {{company_name}}, and we want you to know from the outset that this is a partnership we take seriously.

We go well beyond filling roles. From the moment we receive a brief, we work to understand your business, your culture and the people who will genuinely thrive in your team. Every candidate we put forward has been properly assessed and personally briefed, because the way a candidate arrives matters just as much as who they are.

## How We Work for You

- **Same-day submissions.** When we speak to a strong candidate, their profile is with you that day.
- **Anonymised first look.** Profiles arrive anonymised, so you judge on skills and fit. Full details follow once you want to progress.
- **Prepared candidates.** Every candidate receives a tailored interview preparation pack before they meet you.
- **Pace.** We aim to take a role from brief to start date within 2 weeks, depending on your interview availability.
- **Support after day one.** We check in at Week 1 and Month 1, and every permanent placement carries our 3-month replacement guarantee.

## What Happens Next

1. **Sign your agreements.** You will receive a secure link from clients@live2helprecruitment.co.uk to review and sign our Terms of Engagement and Data Sharing Agreement online. It takes around five minutes.
2. **Tell us about your site.** A short online form captures parking, reception, dress code and key contacts, so every candidate arrives on time and prepared.
3. **Kick-off call.** We will agree your first brief, salary range and interview availability.
4. **A little something.** Keep an eye out for a small welcome gift from the team.
