## About Live 2 Help

Live 2 Help Recruitment delivers a premium, end-to-end placement service across the UK. We do not simply source candidates and hand them over. We manage the full journey, from the first brief to your new hire's first day and beyond, so the fee you pay is an investment you can see.

We recruit across Engineering, Manufacturing, Technical, Sales, Operations, Supply Chain, Finance, Technology and Office roles, with the same rigour at every level.

## Permanent Recruitment

Fees are a percentage of the candidate's agreed starting gross annual salary, tiered by band to reflect the sourcing and assessment involved.

| Salary Band | Placement Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £22,000 | 12% |
| £22,001 to £30,000 | 15% |
| £30,001 to £40,000 | 20% |
| £40,001 and above | 25% |

A salary exactly on a band boundary takes the lower rate. For example, a £30,000 role is charged at 15%.

## Retained Search - Senior and Director Roles

For senior leadership, director-level and specialist roles, a retained search gives you priority access to our full sourcing capacity and exclusivity of effort on your brief.

| Stage | When Payable | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Commitment Fee | On instruction | 10% of agreed salary |
| Placement Fee | On the candidate's start date | 15% of agreed salary |
| **Total** | | **25% of agreed salary** |

The commitment fee is non-refundable and is credited against the total fee on a successful hire.

## Temporary Placements - Contracts of up to 3 Months

Choose one of two options at instruction. Your choice is confirmed in writing and stays fixed once the candidate starts.

**Option A - Fixed Placement Fee**

One fee, invoiced at the end of the candidate's second week, with no further charges for the length of the contract. If the candidate leaves before then, there is no fee and we provide a free replacement.

| Annual Equivalent Salary | Fixed Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £25,000 | £2,500 |
| £25,001 to £35,000 | £3,500 |
| £35,001 and above | £5,000 |

**Option B - Monthly Fee**

A fee invoiced at the start of each calendar month. No replacement is included and fees already paid are non-refundable.

| Annual Equivalent Salary | Monthly Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £25,000 | £1,000 per month |
| £25,001 to £35,000 | £1,200 per month |
| £35,001 and above | £1,500 per month |

**Converting to permanent**

Under either option, converting the candidate to a permanent employee carries a one-off conversion fee. Under Option B, it replaces any further monthly invoices.

| Annual Equivalent Salary | Conversion Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £25,000 | £1,500 |
| £25,001 to £35,000 | £2,000 |
| £35,001 and above | £3,000 |

**Which option suits you?**

| | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|
| £30,000 contract, runs the full 3 months | £3,500 | £3,600 |
| Same contract, converted to permanent after 2 months | £5,500 | £4,400 |
| Free replacement if the candidate leaves early | Yes | No |

Option A is the lower-cost, lower-risk choice when the contract runs its full term. Option B suits clients who expect to convert the candidate early.

## 3-Month Replacement Guarantee

If a permanent candidate we place leaves, or is dismissed for performance, within three months of starting, we run one full replacement search at no extra cost, provided that:

- the original invoice was paid in full within terms;
- you tell us in writing within 7 days of the candidate's last working day; and
- the departure is not due to a change in role, redundancy or restructure.
