## 1. About this Schedule

1.1 This Schedule applies when the Client takes up a Talent Partnership plan. It forms part of, and should be read together with, the Live 2 Help Terms of Engagement (the "Terms").

1.2 Words defined in the Terms have the same meaning in this Schedule. Where this Schedule and the Terms conflict on a Covered Role, this Schedule applies. In every other respect the Terms apply in full.

1.3 The Plan, start date and scope chosen by the Client are recorded in the Plan Order at the end of this Schedule.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Basic Salary | The Applicant's gross annual basic salary for the first 12 months of the Engagement, excluding bonus, commission and benefits. |
| Commencement Date | The date the Plan starts, as set out in the Plan Order. |
| Confirmed Brief | A written role brief, agreed by both parties, setting out the job title, location, Basic Salary range and essential requirements. |
| Covered Role | A permanent vacancy within the Agreed Scope with a Basic Salary of £50,000 or less. |
| Agreed Scope | The role categories, departments and sites set out in the Plan Order. |
| Hire | An Applicant introduced by the Agency who starts a permanent Engagement in a Covered Role. |
| Hire Allowance | The number of Hire Units included in the Plan for each Plan Year. |
| Hire Unit | The measure used to count Hires against the Hire Allowance, as set out in clause 6. |
| Live Role | A Covered Role the Agency is actively working on, from the date of its Confirmed Brief until the Hire's start date or the role's withdrawal. |
| Overage Fee | The fee for a Hire made after the Hire Allowance for the Plan Year has been used, as set out in clause 7. |
| Plan | The Talent Partnership plan selected in the Plan Order. |
| Plan Fee | The monthly fee for the Plan, as set out in clause 3. |
| Plan Year | Each 12-month period starting on the Commencement Date or an anniversary of it. |
| Term | The period of 12 months from the Commencement Date, or a renewal period agreed under clause 4.5. |

## 3. Plans and Fees

3.1 The Plans are:

| Plan | Hire Allowance per Plan Year | Live Roles at one time | Plan Fee per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partner | No allowance (see clause 3.2) | 2 | £495, plus a Fee per Hire |
| Core | 10 Hire Units | 3 | £3,950 |
| Growth | 20 Hire Units | 5 | £7,350 |
| Scale | 30 Hire Units | 8 | £10,200 |
| Enterprise | As agreed in the Plan Order | As agreed | As agreed |

3.2 On the Partner Plan, each Hire is charged at 10% of Basic Salary for up to 6 Hire Units in each Plan Year. Any further Hire in that Plan Year is charged at the Fee in clause 3.3 of the Terms. Each Fee is invoiced on the Hire's start date.

3.3 All Plan Fees, Overage Fees and other Fees are exclusive of VAT, in line with clause 3.8 of the Terms.

## 4. Term, Billing and Renewal

4.1 The Plan runs for the Term. Plan Fees are invoiced monthly in advance on the Commencement Date and on the same date each month after that.

4.2 The Client may instead pay the Plan Fees for the full Term in advance, in which case a 5% discount applies to the Plan Fees. The discount does not apply to Overage Fees or Partner Plan Hire Fees.

4.3 Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date. Clauses 6.2 to 6.4 of the Terms apply to all invoices under this Schedule.

4.4 If a Plan Fee is more than 14 days overdue, the Agency may pause work on all Live Roles until the account is up to date. Plan Fees remain payable during any pause.

4.5 At least 30 days before the end of the Term, the Agency will send the Client a renewal proposal. The Plan ends at the end of the Term unless both parties agree a renewal in writing.

## 5. Break Clause and Early Termination

5.1 Either party may end the Plan by written notice given within the first 90 days of the Term (the "Break Period"). The Plan ends on the date the notice is received.

5.2 If the Plan ends under clause 5.1, each Hire with a start date during the Term is charged at the Fee in clause 3.3 of the Terms. The Plan Fees and any Partner Plan Hire Fees already paid are deducted from the total. If the amount already paid is greater, no refund is due.

5.3 After the Break Period, the Client may not end the Plan before the end of the Term. If the Client stops using the service, the Plan Fees for the rest of the Term remain payable.

5.4 Either party may end the Plan by written notice if the other commits a material breach of this Schedule or the Terms and does not remedy it within 30 days of written notice describing the breach. If the Client ends the Plan because of the Agency's uncured material breach, no further Plan Fees are payable after the date of termination.

## 6. Hire Allowance and Hire Units

6.1 A Hire counts against the Hire Allowance on the Hire's start date, not on offer or acceptance.

6.2 Each Hire uses Hire Units according to Basic Salary:

| Basic Salary | Hire Units used |
|---|---|
| Up to and including £22,000 | 0.5 |
| £22,001 to £40,000 | 1 |
| £40,001 to £50,000 | 1.5 |

6.3 Roles with a Basic Salary above £50,000 are not Covered Roles. They are charged under clause 3 or clause 4 of the Terms and do not use the Hire Allowance.

6.4 The Hire Allowance may be used at any pace during the Plan Year, subject to the Live Role limit in clause 8. Unused Hire Units do not carry into the next Plan Year except under clause 11.

## 7. Overage

7.1 Once the Hire Allowance for a Plan Year has been used, each further Hire is charged an Overage Fee of 15% of Basic Salary.

7.2 Overage Fees are invoiced on the Hire's start date. Where a Hire would take the Client over the Hire Allowance part way through its Hire Units, the Overage Fee is charged on the part of the Hire that falls outside the allowance.

7.3 Overage does not apply to the Partner Plan, which is covered by clause 3.2.

## 8. Live Roles

8.1 The Agency will work on up to the number of Live Roles set out for the Plan in clause 3.1 at any one time.

8.2 Where the Client has more open Covered Roles than the Plan allows, the extra roles are held in a queue and begin when a Live Role closes. The Client may upgrade under clause 13 to increase the limit.

## 9. Service Levels

9.1 The Agency will provide a first shortlist of screened Applicants within 7 Business Days of each Confirmed Brief.

9.2 If the Agency misses the shortlist deadline for a role, the Client may, by written notice, release that role to another supplier. The released role stops being a Live Role. If the Client later engages an Applicant the Agency introduced for that role, it counts as a Hire.

9.3 The Client will give feedback on shortlisted Applicants within 3 Business Days and offer interview slots within 5 Business Days of a request. Where the Client cannot, the deadline in clause 9.1 is extended by the same amount of time.

9.4 The Agency will provide access to the client portal showing Live Roles, pipeline progress and Hire Units used, and will hold a hiring review with the Client each quarter.

## 10. Exclusivity

10.1 During the Term, the Client will instruct the Agency exclusively on all permanent vacancies within the Agreed Scope, and will not instruct another recruitment agency on them, except for roles released under clause 9.2.

10.2 The following are excluded from exclusivity and do not use the Hire Allowance: internal promotions and transfers, and hires made through the Client's own staff referral scheme.

10.3 If the Client fills a Covered Role through another recruitment agency in breach of clause 10.1, that appointment counts against the Hire Allowance as if it were a Hire, or is charged as an Overage Fee if the Hire Allowance has been used.

## 11. Rollover Credit

11.1 If the Client renews the Plan within 30 days of the end of the Term, unused Hire Units from the final Plan Year carry into the renewed Term as extra Hire Units.

11.2 The rollover is limited to 25% of the Plan's Hire Allowance, rounded down to the nearest whole Hire Unit. Rollover credit has no cash value and does not apply to the Partner Plan.

## 12. Withdrawn Roles and the Replacement Guarantee

12.1 If the Client withdraws a Covered Role before an Applicant accepts an offer, no Hire Units are used.

12.2 If the Client withdraws an accepted offer before the Applicant's start date, 0.5 Hire Units are used. On the Partner Plan, or where the Hire Allowance has been used, clause 8 of the Terms applies instead, calculated on the Fee that would have applied under this Schedule. This clause replaces clause 8 of the Terms for all other Covered Roles.

12.3 The replacement guarantee in clause 7 of the Terms applies to every Hire. A replacement Applicant provided under that guarantee does not use any Hire Units and does not attract a further Fee.

## 13. Changing Plan

13.1 The Client may upgrade to a higher Plan at any time. The upgrade takes effect from the next billing date and starts a new Term on the new Plan. Hire Units already used in the current Plan Year count towards the new Hire Allowance.

13.2 The Client may move to a lower Plan only at renewal.

## 14. Exclusions and After the Plan Ends

14.1 Temporary Placements (clause 5 of the Terms), Retained Searches (clause 4 of the Terms) and roles above £50,000 Basic Salary sit outside the Plans and are charged under the Terms.

14.2 Clause 9 of the Terms continues to apply after the Plan ends. If the Client engages an Applicant introduced during the Term within 12 months of the Plan ending, the Fee in clause 3.3 of the Terms is payable.
