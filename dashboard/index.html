Data and GDPR

How consent and review dates work

Consent record. This shows when and how a candidate agreed to us holding their details. Sending an email template does not create one. It is recorded in three ways: automatically for new application form candidates (switch below), by ticking the consent box when you log a contact, or by hand in the Candidate Pool dialog.

Review date. This moves forward by itself. It is always 12 months after the last activity on a record (a stage change, note, contact or interview), so only candidates who have gone quiet ever come up for review. Extend 12 months is an optional override that restarts the clock from today.

Many agencies rely on legitimate interests rather than consent for candidate data, so check with your adviser which applies to you.
